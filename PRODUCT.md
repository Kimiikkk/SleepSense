# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People reviewing their own sleep data.

## Product Purpose

SleepSense presents sleep patterns and general wellness guidance. It is currently a software prototype, and wearable readings are simulated.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The app provides account creation, sign-in, a profile, generated sleep sessions, sleep-stage and sensor visualizations, and an AI sleep assistant. Current readings and generated sessions are simulated; the assistant provides general wellness information and does not diagnose medical conditions.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the SleepSense name and all existing screens and features. The user pinned the provided wearable-app screenshot as the visual reference: a near-black device display, compact outlined cards, bright accent colors, circular readouts, and matching horizontal meters. Adapt that visual language to SleepSense's actual sleep parameters rather than fitness metrics.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing Spring Boot and Thymeleaf application is the product demonstration. It contains simulated sleep duration, quality, sleep stages, EEG bands, heart rate, movement, SpO₂, skin temperature, and assistant responses. Do not present prototype readings as measured health data.

## Product Principles

- Make personal sleep information easy to scan and understand.
- Keep simulated readings clearly identified as simulated.
- Keep wellness information distinct from medical diagnosis.
- Preserve the existing account and sleep review workflows.
